With all [the recent karaoke-specific upgrades added to DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) over the last few months it’s no wonder the do-it-all software has become just as popular for hosting professional karaoke events as Karaoki.

Complete with a true [managed singer rotation list](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), [singer/song history](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), and [automatic filler music player](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), in additional to all the prior exiting karaoke features like [Party Tyme Karaoke](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) support, it’s now easy for versatile DJs to transition seamlessly from DJs/Video mixing to hosting karaoke. No need to launch Karaoki or another standalone karaoke player — [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) has you covered, soup to nuts.

DEX 3’s skin is multifaceted and robust with on-screen tabs for 2-deck, 4-deck, simple and variations of each (with/without video, sampler decks, etc). What doesn’t currently install with the application, at least for now, is a basic karaoke-specific skin.

Since we’ve been getting asked for it and we recently created a DEX 3-centric skin for LYRX, our standalone karaoke software for MAC, we wanted to make the skin available for use with DEX 3 now. Although we do plan to include the skin with the next free [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) update.
